About Me

Researcher & Photographer

Postdoctoral Researcher at RIKEN CPR, developing ML-enhanced molecular models at the frontier of computational biophysics and soft matter.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at RIKEN Cluster for Pioneering Research (CPR), Wako, Japan, working in the Theoretical Molecular Science Laboratory with Prof. Yuji Sugita. My research centers on developing machine-learning-enhanced coarse-grained force fields for intrinsically disordered proteins and polymer systems.

My doctoral work at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) Bengaluru established original multi-scale simulation frameworks for dendrimer-based systems across three high-impact domains: energy storage (supercapacitors in Physical Review Applied), nuclear waste remediation (uranyl extraction in Langmuir), and targeted drug delivery (siRNA complexation and cytotoxicity prediction in ACS Omega).

I combine physics-based simulation (MD, MC, DFT) with data-driven approaches (neural networks, CNNs, ensemble ML) to extract predictive structure–property relationships. I hold top All-India competitive examination ranks: JEST AIR 8, JAM AIR 19, CSIR-UGC NET JRF AIR 25, GATE AIR 33.
